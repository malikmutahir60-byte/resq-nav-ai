# Safety & Responsible AI

RESQ-NAV AI is a research prototype.

## Human Oversight

Critical rescue decisions must remain under trained human supervision.

## Known Limitations

- Computer vision may produce false detections.
- Sensors can fail.
- Communication may be unavailable.
- UAV battery capacity is limited.
- Environmental conditions can reduce detection accuracy.
- AI-generated explanations may be imperfect.

## Safety Measures

- Human approval for critical actions
- Emergency stop capability
- Conservative route planning
- Confidence thresholds
- Clear indication of uncertain detections
- Logging of system decisions

## Deployment Disclaimer

RESQ-NAV AI is not certified for autonomous real-world emergency deployment.
