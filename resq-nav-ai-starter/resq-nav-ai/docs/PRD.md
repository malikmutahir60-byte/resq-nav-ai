# RESQ-NAV AI — Product Requirements Document

## Product Vision

Enable emergency responders to obtain rapid situational awareness and safer route recommendations from AI-assisted UAV systems operating in GPS-denied disaster environments.

## Problem

Disasters can destroy communication infrastructure, obstruct physical access and make conventional search-and-rescue operations dangerous and slow.

## Target Users

- Search-and-rescue teams
- Emergency responders
- Disaster-management organizations
- Industrial safety teams
- Infrastructure inspection teams

## Core Features

1. Survivor detection
2. Obstacle detection
3. Disaster environment mapping
4. BFS search
5. A* navigation
6. Risk-aware route planning
7. Rescue priority analysis
8. Dynamic route recalculation
9. AI Rescue Copilot

## MVP

The MVP will demonstrate these capabilities through a simulated UAV operating in a GPS-denied disaster environment.

## Non-Goals

The MVP does not attempt to provide certified autonomous emergency response or replace trained rescue professionals.

## Success Criteria

The prototype should:

- Successfully navigate obstacle-filled environments
- Detect simulated or visual survivor targets
- Compare BFS and A* navigation
- Generate risk-aware routes
- Recalculate routes after environmental changes
- Provide understandable AI explanations
- Be accessible through a live web application
